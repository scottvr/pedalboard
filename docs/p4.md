# ESP32-P4: the project after the kit

Notes from the 2026-10-12 evening conversation, written down 2026-10-13. Nothing here is
started; it is the shape of the next project and the facts gathered so far. The LAFVIN kit
work in `BACKLOG.md` comes first and does not depend on any of it.

## The idea

NAM on an ESP32-P4. The odds of the pedal running on one are good, and of running better than
on the S3 decent, because the chip removes the two walls the S3 port keeps hitting:

- **Cores.** Two RISC-V HP cores at 400 MHz with single-precision FPUs and a 128-bit SIMD
  extension that Espressif's DSP library already uses on the P4. The S3 does the same work at
  240 MHz on two LX7 cores at about 87% of the 1,333 µs block. The LP core (40 MHz) is for
  sleep-time housekeeping: it could own the footswitch and LEDs, not a sample of audio.
- **Memory.** About 768 KB of internal SRAM with no IRAM-versus-DRAM trade, plus PSRAM on a
  faster bus (32 MB on the boards below). Most of the pedal's memory document, the bank
  scheme, the IRAM reclaims and the arena lending exist because the S3 has 512 KB split
  awkwardly; on the P4 that largely dissolves. PSRAM is still slower than internal SRAM, so
  the hot path still wants to live in L2MEM.
- **USB.** High-Speed host. The pedal's whole UAC story is shaped by the S3 being Full Speed
  only; on the P4 the Scarlett and MOTU class of interfaces come into range and UAC2 at 24-bit
  stereo stops being a capacity exercise.
- **Flash.** 16 to 32 MB on the boards below, so the OTA slots stop being a budget line (the
  current LAFVIN image fills about 60% of a 4 MB slot).

The real cost is the NAM kernels. The pedal's hot path is hand-written Xtensa assembly (the
mixed-precision block code and the packed PCM routines) and none of it moves to RISC-V. That
is a rewrite against the P4's vector unit, which is also the chance to beat the S3 rather than
match it. Everything above the kernels (effects graph, storage, UI, maintenance) is C++ and
TSX and moves.

The P4 has no radio. Its boards pair it with an ESP32-C6 over SDIO and Wi-Fi goes through
Espressif's remote-Wi-Fi layer (`esp_wifi_remote`), so the maintenance link is a different
code path from today's on-chip driver: the one place beyond the kernels where the pedal's
maintenance service needs real changes.

## Where Geastack stands on the P4

- The targets package carries three P4 board directories: `esp32-p4-m5stack-tab5` and
  `esp32-p4-waveshare-touch-lcd-7` (ILI9881 panels over MIPI-DSI, GT911 touch) and
  `esp32-p4-waveshare-touch-lcd-3.5` (ST7796). The CLI's board catalog lists them and flashes
  P4 over USB Serial/JTAG at 921600.
- The maintainer said on 2026-10-12 that he owns no P4 hardware. So those directories were
  written from vendor board support packages and have never run: the DSI display pipeline and
  the remote Wi-Fi layer are unverified assumptions in that corner of the tree. "Nobody has
  tried" is the honest status, and the first boot of any P4 board here is also Gea's first
  P4 run.
- The composer has one base, the S3. A new P4 board today is a hand-written target directory
  like those three, not a JSON definition.
- The maintainer also said: "[the P4] is a different architecture not currently supported by
  coyopedal, and I have made the decision not to open source it myself." Read as a statement
  about his own work (he has P4 work and is keeping it closed, which the Gea embedded
  targets' commercial-licence path exists for), not as a line against ours: coyopedal and the
  targets are GPLv3 and a port in the open is allowed. Practically it means an open P4 port
  would probably not be merged upstream. Before starting, ask him the courtesy question,
  about his plans rather than ours: is an open P4 port something he would rather not see, or
  just something he would not merge. The umbrella repo is the home for a port he does not
  take.

## Sequence, when the kit is done

1. First light of a Gea P4 target on a board on hand (blink, then the C6 link). Gea's first
   P4 run.
2. Headless pedal boot on the POE-ETH board with the ES8311 path, kernels stubbed or scalar.
   Both boards on hand are panel-less, so this is the LAFVIN headless-first route again, with
   a hand-written P4 target directory (the composer has no P4 base).
3. NAM kernels on the RISC-V vector unit, measured against the S3's 87%.
4. Maintenance over Ethernet first (one PoE cable), `esp_wifi_remote` second. Display and touch
   only if a DSI panel is added or the Waveshare 4.3 panel board is bought.
